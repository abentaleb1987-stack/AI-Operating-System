The most important feature is still missing: **a proper read-aloud mode**.

For me, this isn't worth switching to yet because I don't want to read every long response word by word. That creates unnecessary cognitive load. I'd rather use my mental energy to analyze and think about the content instead of just consuming it.

A good text-to-speech experience would make a huge difference. Ideally it should include:

* Play / Pause
* Adjustable playback speed
* The ability to select and read only a specific paragraph or section
* The option to keep listening while working in other apps

Gemini already does some of this, and it makes long-form AI much more efficient to use.

As ChatGPT becomes more of a work assistant and agent, reducing cognitive load is just as important as adding new capabilities. A flexible read-aloud feature would be one of the biggest usability improvements.
