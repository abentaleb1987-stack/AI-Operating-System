@iebschool
il y a 14 heures
For years we've organized our work around tools: opening email, finding the document, checking the calendar, going back to the chat.

What's interesting about plugins is that they reverse that logic. Now the tool can come to the context, instead of forcing us to chase it through five different applications.

That change may seem small, but it significantly improves the way we work.

In our training programs, we emphasize this principle: it's not just about knowing how to connect tools, but about understanding their purpose, what information should be shared, and what boundaries shouldn't be crossed.

Because the more context an AI has, the more useful it can be, but also the more important it becomes to protect privacy, security, and data quality.

The advantage lies not in connecting everything for the sake of connecting it, but in doing so with intention, control, and responsibility.
